# Neko Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Neko Bot ("the Bot") collects, processes, stores, and uses information when operating within Discord servers.

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide the Bot's functionality.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled and used, Neko Bot may process the following information:

### Discord Information
- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server IDs.
- Channel IDs.
- Usernames, display names, and avatars where needed.

### Activity and Statistics
- Message counts and activity timestamps.
- Image and attachment counts.
- Voice channel activity and session durations.
- Experience points, levels, and leaderboard rankings.
- Sakura balances, pickups, and achievements.
- Weekly quest progress.

### Bot Features
- Command interactions and related information.
- Messages and conversation context provided to the AI chat feature.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

- Operate commands and provide Bot functionality.
- Calculate activity statistics, XP, and leaderboards.
- Maintain Sakura balances, achievements, and quest progress.
- Deliver reminders and manage AFK functionality.
- Generate AI responses when the AI feature is used.
- Maintain service reliability and prevent abuse.

## 4. Message Content

Neko Bot may access message content when necessary to operate features such as activity tracking, Sakura drops, and AI chat.

Message content used for AI interactions may be temporarily retained as conversation context.

The Bot does not intentionally collect message content unrelated to its functionality.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in databases or files used by the Bot.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access.

However, no storage system or internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Neko Bot operates through Discord and may use external services for AI responses, music functionality, and other features.

Information necessary to fulfill a request may be transmitted to these providers.

Such providers process information according to their respective privacy policies.

## 7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, maintain relevant features, or meet applicable obligations.

Certain information, such as statistics and game progress, may remain stored until it is deleted or no longer required.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Neko Bot is intended for individuals permitted to use Discord under Discord's age requirements and applicable laws.

The Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not eligible to use Discord.
